# Wi-Fi bands and channels

The router broadcasts on 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz. Band steering moves capable devices to 5 GHz automatically.
To split the bands, open Settings, choose Wi-Fi, and turn off Smart Connect. You can then name each band separately.

Channel selection is automatic. To pick a channel manually, disable Auto Channel and choose 1, 6 or 11 on 2.4 GHz.
